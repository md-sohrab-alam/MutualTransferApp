# Privacy Policy — Mutual Teacher Transfer (Unofficial)

**Last updated:** January 2025

This app is an independent tool to help Bihar government school teachers connect for mutual transfer. It is not an official government app and has no affiliation with the Bihar Government or its Education Department.

## Data We Collect
- Account and profile details you provide (e.g., name, post level, current and preferred district/block/school, contact method you choose to share).
- App telemetry (crash logs and basic analytics) to improve reliability.

## How We Use Data
- To match teachers who are mutually interested in transfers.
- To communicate app status or critical updates (if you opt in).

## Data Sharing
We do not sell your data. We may use essential service providers (e.g., hosting, analytics, crash reporting) under contracts that restrict processing to our instructions.

## Data Storage & Security
Data is stored securely using industry-standard practices. While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## Your Choices
- You can update or delete your profile data from within the app (or by contacting us).
- You can request account deletion by contacting: **iamsohrabalam@gmail.com**.

## Children's Privacy
This app is intended for adult teaching professionals.

## Government Affiliation
This app does not represent, endorse, or have any affiliation with the Bihar Government or its Education Department. All information in the app is user-submitted; no official government transfer data is displayed.

## Official Government Sources
For official education information, please refer to:
- Bihar State Portal: https://state.bihar.gov.in/
- Bihar Education Online Services: https://edu-online.bihar.gov.in/
- Education Department (Bihar): https://education.bih.nic.in/
- SCERT Bihar: https://scert.bihar.gov.in/

Always confirm any transfer through your District Education Office or other official channels.

## Contact
